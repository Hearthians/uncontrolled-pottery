# Hand Tracking Assets

Hand Landmarker model: Google MediaPipe, float16 version 1.
Source: https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/hand_landmarker/hand_landmarker/float16/1/hand_landmarker.task

WASM runtime: @mediapipe/tasks-vision 0.10.32, distributed under Apache-2.0.
Source: https://www.npmjs.com/package/@mediapipe/tasks-vision
License: https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

Assets are served locally. Camera frames are processed in the browser and are not uploaded or saved.
