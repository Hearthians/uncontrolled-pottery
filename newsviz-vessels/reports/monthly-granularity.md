## How to read the verdict

Monthly means use only months with **5 or more** articles, so a handful of stories cannot swing the comparison. Between-year standard deviations of annual means (21 years with at least one article; 2006 is empty) are:

| Dimension | Between-year std of annual means | Range of annual means |
|---|---:|---:|
| valence | 0.080 | 0.308 |
| alarmist | 0.116 | 0.390 |
| angry | 0.063 | 0.221 |

A year is:

- **too-sparse** if fewer than 4 months have 5+ articles;
- **distinct** if at least two dimensions have a monthly-mean range ≥ 0.10, or one dimension has range ≥ 0.15, or the std of monthly means is ≥ 0.04 and at least 60% of the between-year std;
- **mild** if only one dimension crosses the 0.10 range bar (and that range is under 0.15);
- **flat** if no dimension meets those bars.

These cutoffs are descriptive, not a significance test. Sparse months still have real articles; they are just too thin to estimate a monthly mean.

## Design implications

- **Months do differ, but only in the busy years.** Distinct: [2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2024, 2026]. Mild: [2025]. Flat: [2023]. Too sparse to judge: [2005, 2006, 2007, 2008, 2009, 2010, 2011, 2012, 2013].
- **2005–2014 and 2020 are too thin for monthly means.** Several of these years have only one- or two-digit article counts and many empty months. A 12-rib vessel will correctly look almost plain, with one or two ribs carrying a handful of articles. For these years the annual vessel remains the clearer summary.
- **The busiest month is 2026-09, with 243 articles**, which exceeds a 117-wall-layer budget. Score mode must quantile-sample those months. Date mode does not: it aggregates by day-of-month (at most 31 bands per rib), which stays well under 117 layers.
- **2023 is high-volume but flat across months** (monthly-mean ranges 0.055 valence / 0.091 alarmist / 0.049 angry), yet the article-level std stays large (0.170 / 0.224 / 0.193). Month averages agree; days inside a month still move. Date-order ribs can show that within-month texture, which a single annual column hides.
- **Daily layers for a whole year are not useful.** Even 2025–2026 leave a large share of calendar days empty, and busy days already bundle several articles. The preview should map *day-of-month to height on that month's rib*: empty days stay stable, several stories on one day share a short band (mean score), and empty months are a fully stable rib with a visible gutter.
- **Default encoding:** 12 circumferential ribs (Jan–Dec), 120 layers (3-layer solid base + 117 wall). Height in monthly-date mode is time within the month, not severity, so anomalies can appear mid-vessel instead of stacking into a column. A 'by score' rib mode and an annual (sorted, no ribs) mode are available to compare against the existing preview.
- **Valence, alarmist and angry do not always peak in the same month.** When they diverge, the three vessels will disagree about which rib is most disturbed — that is a feature, not a defect.
